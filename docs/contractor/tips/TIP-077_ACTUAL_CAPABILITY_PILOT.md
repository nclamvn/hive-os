## Submission, checking and falsifiable outcomes

Each first admitted arm stays in the denominator even if it never submits.
Preserve original final proof bytes, all visible attempts, prompt/source digests,
request/session identity, timestamps, interruptions and transfer durations before
any grader sees an answer. No post-verdict repair in this cohort.

Grade without arm labels, using the same preregistered rubric:

1. Faithful statement and all assumptions/quantifiers.
2. Correct necessity and sufficiency (where classification is required).
3. Every blocking lemma justified, no circular implication.
4. Boundary/equality/degenerate cases covered.
5. Universal claim follows from proof, not finite numerical testing.
6. Clear final scope and absence of an unresolved essential gap.

Grades: INFORMALLY_REVIEWED_SUPPORTED, ESSENTIAL_GAP, INCORRECT,
NO_SUBMISSION, or REVIEW_INCONCLUSIVE. Record specific disputed steps and reference
comparison. Same-model independent context is NOT an independent mathematical
human, a different-model robustness panel, or a formal proof checker. No result
here mints VERIFIED_EXACT_SCOPE for a universal theorem or an official IMO score.
An unresolved reviewer disagreement means REVIEW_INCONCLUSIVE, not majority PASS.

Report all six outcomes, paired differences, actual observed requests/tools/time,
all unknown usage and every intervention. Provisional descriptive success means
at least one complete proof is supported by independent review; a local method
lead is only a positive paired difference under the visible envelope. It is not
statistical/population superiority, novelty, open-problem ability, or real-user
value. A zero difference or loss is a valid result and must not trigger new tasks.
