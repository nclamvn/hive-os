## R01 — Quantified sufficient proof and allocation

Each row p,m has integral T=(p+m)/4, gcd(T,m)=1 and p>m. Recheck prime p,
p1mod840, and m either a registered prime3mod4 or3q with prime q1mod4.
Factor T exactly into sorted distinct prime blocks r_i^e_i. Use G=U(m),
target t=-1, group order M=m-1 or2(q-1). No character-based eligibility filter.

First allocate support J to a constructive subgroup chain K0={1}. At each
step choose the smallest-prime unused factor whose exact relative order
d=ord(r_i K in G/K) is at most2e_i+1. Include order1 factors. Retain exact
K and legal exponent representations; this is a covered subgroup, NOT an
exact stabilizer of the final image. Extend K with d cosets using consecutive
digits [-e_i,-e_i+d-1]. Reconsider skipped factors after growth. Stop if none
eligible. Each factor/valuation is consumed once. If t in covered K, issue
CHAIN_CERTIFIED with a legal constructive representation.

If t absent from K and at least two unused factors remain, enumerate all
unordered nonempty proper bipartitions of unused prime support exactly once:
bitmask ascending, fixing the first unused index in A; complement is B.
No empty/full half-box, no overlapping support and no reused chain factor.
For each half, map its signed assignments to G/K (canonical coset representative
is minimum numeric element of gK). Let mu count assignments per coset,
N=product(2e_i+1), E=sum mu^2, L=ceil(N^2/E), all exact integers.
If L_A+L_B>M/|K|, pigeonhole forces the target coset in the product. Select
deterministic matching half representatives, then lift the discrepancy once
using the separately allocated covered K. The resulting combined exponents
remain within actual valuations. Issue ENERGY_CERTIFIED only after checking
the exact root-bound coprime pair and strict Type-II identity.

The proof is Cauchy–Schwarz plus strict cardinality intersection in the SAME
quotient, then a constructive lift. Existing collision-kernel note already
proves weighted energy; do not relabel it as a new theorem or assume exact
energy is cheaper. Certified upper bounds on E are safe; sampled/lower bounds
are not. No new cyclic-block Kneser strategy in this implementation; it may
be investigated as a proof-only future proposal, never posthoc fallback.

If neither sufficient method triggers, return NOT_CERTIFIED, never MISS,
root-excluded or no-solution. Explicit false-variant controls: generated-group
membership without saturation; even K excluding composite target; equality
instead of strict >; reused support; wrong energy/omitted diagonal; free
exact-stabilizer budget; reused chain factors; invalid quotient lift.
