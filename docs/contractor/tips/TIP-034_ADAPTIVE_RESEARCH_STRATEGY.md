## Architecture and requirements

Separate immutable evidence/integrity checks from an explicitly versioned, revisable selection policy. New eligible cycles retain a root-bound literature/method packet before planning, and give that same packet to every actual worker role. Old cycles remain readable under their original rules.

Approved implementation seams: pin the new policy/root in the trusted CommandRecord outcome and mirror it in the outbox at cycle creation, without a schema migration. Because event jobId requires a real DurableJob, append the policy declaration as the FIRST event on its first legitimate claimed job, not against a fabricated job at request creation. Empty queued traces are valid; any call or portfolio requires declaration and packet. Tenant-scoped command-derived expected policy is required by both execution and read validation. Rehashed event stripping must not turn a new-policy cycle into a legacy cycle. Historical freshness is evaluated at its recorded admission boundary, not regraded against today's clock. Selection ranks scientific relevance, then executes the best eligible READY direction while retaining higher-ranked DEFERRED tool obligations with an explanation. It stops `METHOD_DEFERRED` only when no eligible safe direction remains; no arbitrary model-written tool is executed. This avoids both tool-first bias and unnecessary human intervention for every missing runtime.

## Acceptance scenarios

- Given an old V1 portfolio/trace, validation and display preserve its original digest and semantics; adding the new policy does not silently regrade history.
- Given a new eligible cycle, the strategy evidence is retained before the first planner call and all actual role callbacks receive its exact root-bound digest.
- Given arXiv 2609.09204 v1/HTML/PDF/DOI alias, a retained latest-v2 withdrawal excludes its results as accepted premises even if AI reviewers agree; independent arithmetic findings are not automatically invalidated.
- Given failed, oversized, redirected, malformed or stale source retrieval, retain unavailable/unverified status; never fabricate a successful current check or execute URLs suggested by a model.
- Given two candidates, one with a ready easy survey and one addressing an explicit structural bridge, availability alone cannot select the easy survey. Tool gaps are deferred visibly and never bypass safe-execution checks.
- Given a refuted/repeated branch or an inconclusive result, subsequent selection and its replayable rationale reflect the outcome rather than blindly replaying the old task. Model utility predictions remain proposals, not theorem/probability scores.
- Given a retained method packet/selection altered or rebound to another root, validation fails before downstream calls. Legacy-only admission cannot be used to strip the marker from a new-policy cycle.
- UI reads retained data only, escapes source text, wraps long fields, labels four old targets as development/warmup rather than held-out success, and creates no theorem badge.
