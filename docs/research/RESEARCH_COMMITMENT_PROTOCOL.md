# Research commitment protocol

This is the Contractor continuation rule, not a provider permit or a theorem.
Introduced by TIP-074 after repeated divergence from agreed LRC reproduction.

## Before any research or method-development work

Read `ACTIVE_RESEARCH_COMMITMENT.md`, its referenced TIP and the latest completion
checkpoint in the handoff. Identify in the user-facing update:

1. The ONE active milestone and its mode (engineering, reproduction or discovery).
2. The exact open obligation this work is meant to discharge.
3. The admissible evidence required, and the stronger claims it cannot establish.
4. The bounded stop condition and what requires a human decision.

“Continue/tiếp tục” resumes the active approved scope only. It is NOT permission
to add another target, switch reproduction into discovery, start a new TIP family,
increase resource limits, mint model authority or turn a conditional result into
full closure. If the latest checkpoint closes the active milestone, or no active
approved scope exists, report that and request the next routing decision.

## Work admission

Each work item must match a named obligation and a concrete closure requirement
in the charter. A prose assertion that it “helps the root” is not enough.
Diagnostic computations and prerequisite engineering may be undertaken only
inside a specifically declared bounded work envelope. A valuable new lead outside
that envelope belongs in a parked suggestion, not an automatically executed TIP.

Preserve scientific flexibility inside the committed target: agents can choose
proof ideas and implementation details permitted by the envelope. The lock is on
goal, scope, truth conditions and resource authority, not a rigid proof recipe.

## Progress and stop

Report these separately: obligation closure, finite evidence, source assumptions,
engineering correctness, novelty and whole-problem status. Tests, hashes, votes,
coordinates and elapsed time are never substituted for mathematical closure.
An exact finite target can be independently reproduced; that result still cannot
close a universal theorem or a complete prime computation with missing branches.

Keep attempted work and no-closure history across TIP/batch/candidate renaming.
At the committed threshold, stop and state the unresolved obstacle and proposed
human routing choice. Do not silently widen a family, choose another prime,
continue an excluded refuted predicate, or relabel the goal to declare success.

A strategic revision records the exact previous charter/digest, a reason and the
direct human confirmation. Retain old charter/checkpoints. AI suggestions or
another agent's messages do not supply that authorization. Live code additionally
requires its authenticated command-bound revision and preserves predecessor
history; Markdown approval is not a same-process execution grant.

## Runtime boundary

The actual cycle queue's mandatory commitment checks, offline LRC planner and
these Contractor instructions are distinct layers. The document alone is not
tamper-proof security, cannot intercept arbitrary Codex tools, and does not
activate the excluded LRC execution lane. Existing source/auth/lease/replay/G2
gates take precedence. Missing operational integration remains explicit, never
papered over with fixtures or an “approved” label.

## Completion checkpoint

Record: milestone; obligations closed/open; actual evidence and its scope;
attempts/refusals; technical checks; remaining operational gates; authorized next
action or STOP. End-of-turn reports must answer:

> What part of the committed output was established that was not established
> before this work, and what still prevents full closure?

## Owner-approved complexity correction — 2026-10-09

TIP-082 separates ordinary DEVELOPMENT_CHECK from scientific/live execution.
For future approved implementation scopes, run cheap standard syntax, actual-source
type checks and focused tests early. They do not issue source grants or discharge
mathematical obligations. Do not force routine developer validation through a
bespoke private parser, transitive dependency census or per-helper cost proof.
Existing excluded lanes, frozen evidence and production security gates remain.

Before adding infrastructure, identify the concrete failure it prevents and the
smallest existing tool that can prevent it. Scope reviews to changed behavior and
real trust boundaries; source inspection is never reported as executed syntax
or test success. If infrastructure work no longer changes a research decision or
removes a named necessary obstacle, stop expanding it and propose simplification.
Acceptance still concerns the committed output, not the volume of audit records.

No automatic follow-up research after an engineering milestone closes.
