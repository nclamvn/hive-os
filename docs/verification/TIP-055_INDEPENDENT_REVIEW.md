## Independently checked universal reasoning

Assume a positive primitive distinct hypothetical counterexample, k>=3, p prime
and no coordinate divisible by p. Reducing modulo p yields one of a finite set
of base classes. A grid witness for its lift at level l is also a witness for
the actual integer tuple: coordinate differences are multiples of lp, whose
products with j/(lp) are integers. This transfers to every lift, not a sampled
integer representative.

If g=gcd(l,other residues)>1, g divides every corresponding actual speed except
possibly one. Primitive normalization forces gcd(g,exception)=1, since any
common divisor would divide the entire tuple. Lower LRC gives a good t0 for
the remaining distinct speed values. Shifts q/g leave those phases unchanged.
More generally, if only g does not divide the exception is known, its orbit has
h=g/gcd(g,exception)>=2 equally spaced points. Such a grid has shortest closed
containing arc1-1/h>=1/2. The bad arc is OPEN and has length2/(k+1)<=1/2.
It cannot contain the grid, even at equality; a boundary is a good witness.
Thus the positive primitive counterexample is impossible in a proper lift.
The generalized g6/exception2 example is valid for this orbit argument but lies
outside primitive full-tuple admission.

A modular gcd-proper leaf can instead have g dividing ALL residues, so its
exception orbit is h1. That leaf is still proper by definition: no primitive
actual counterexample can occur in its integer class. The h>=2 shift condition
must not be imposed on the modular gcd classifier itself or these valid vacuous
exclusions would be lost.

Properness persists at every integer multiple of a level. The old grid embeds
in the finer grid and residue differences preserve phases. An old gcd divisor
still divides both the new level and the new coordinates. Hence an improper
refined tuple must reduce to an improper old tuple. Enumerating every c^k lift
of EVERY retained parent and discarding exactly proper leaves maintains the
complete fiber by induction; counts alone cannot replace that enumeration.

If every base class is eventually proper, pick a terminal level for each of
the finitely many (p-1)^k classes and take their LCM. Each coarse terminal grid/
gcd test survives at that common level, so I at the LCM is empty. Conversely an
empty complete level immediately makes every base class eventually proper.
There is no exchange with an infinite/uniform bound assumption here.

The alternative whole-class branch must establish LR for EVERY positive
pairwise-distinct integer tuple in the class, including nonprimitive tuples
and arbitrarily large lifts. A finite velocity box or a primitive-only theorem
does not supply it. Every base class and every lift must receive a valid branch
certificate before the contraposition yields prime divisibility. Generic third
certificates also need separate symmetry stability; positive-class scope alone
does not justify replacing arbitrary signed tuples by positive representatives.

## Unit/CRT and notation repairs

For transformed residues u'=b*v modulo M, a witness j/M for u' transfers to
b*j/M for v, because the phases are identical modulo integers. The inverse
direction is not interchangeable. Independent control M28,b5,inverse17,
v=(1,2,4),u'=(5,10,20),j2 has correct transported numerator10 GOOD while6 fails.

The restricted CRT choice b=a^-1 modulo p and b=1 modulo l requires gcd(l,p)=1;
it is impossible for p3,l3,a2. General unit invariance can be repaired by writing
l=p^e*m with gcd(m,p)=1, imposing b=a^-1 modulo p and b=1 modulo m. Then b is
coprime to every prime divisor of lp. Multiplication by b preserves gcd tests
and permutes grid times. These are localized proof-exposition repairs, not a
refutation of the lower theorem. Scaling by b need not preserve primitivity,
which is why primitive-only certificates cannot feed the whole-class branch.

Notation is an alias, not a false source formula. Let G=(productv)^(1/k),
Fhat=sqrt(H) and S_source=sumv. Direct normalization gives
G*Fhat=S_source*F_source and R=k/Fhat=kG/(S_source*F_source). The previously
retained covolume and product bounds are identical under this substitution.
