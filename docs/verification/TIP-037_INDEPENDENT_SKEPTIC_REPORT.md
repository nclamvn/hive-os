## Exact scope and proof

Let h be a prime congruent to 3 modulo 4, T a positive integer coprime to h, G its multiplicative group, and S the complete signed prime-factor image with every exponent z_q in [-v_q(T),v_q(T)]. Assume -1 is missing. Let H be the **exact stabilizer of S**, not the subgroup generated by factor residues. Define n=|G|/|H| and V as the sum of valuations of factors whose residue is outside H.

1. Since 1 lies in S and H stabilizes S, H is contained in S. Therefore -1 is not in H. In cyclic G, a subgroup of even order contains the unique element of order two, so |H| is odd. Since |G|=h-1 has exactly one factor of two, n is 2 modulo 4. In particular n is even.
2. The quotient image S/H has trivial stabilizer: an additional quotient period would lift to a period of S outside its exact stabilizer. The quotient target -1H is nonidentity and has order two. A visible factor of quotient order two would hit that target with exponent one; because S is a union of H-cosets, this would put -1 itself in S. Thus no visible factor has quotient order two.
3. Express each visible valuation unit as a virtual atom {1,qH,q^-1H}. It has three distinct points: visible order one is excluded by definition, and order two by the preceding argument. Repeated atoms still give precisely the capped signed exponent image; cancellation becomes unused factor mass in k, not a prime illegally shared between coprime u and v. Invisible factors become singleton identity atoms in the quotient.
4. Every intermediate period persists when further atoms are added, hence is contained in the final quotient stabilizer and is trivial. The aperiodic case of [Kneser's theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/1303.3539v1), iterated over V atoms, gives |S/H|>=2V+1. The target coset is absent, so |S/H|<=n-1. Consequently **2V<=n-2**. This is a necessary condition for a miss, not a sufficient miss classifier or a proof of a hit whenever the inequality holds.
5. For a visible factor q^e, if its quotient order r were <=2e+1, its single-factor exponent interval [-e,e] would cover the entire cyclic subgroup generated by qH. That subgroup would be a period of the final quotient image, contradicting aperiodicity. Therefore the strict gap **ord(qH)>2e+1** holds for each visible factor. Comparing e to the order in G rather than G/H would weaken and misstate the required certificate.
6. Because H has odd order, it lies in the quadratic-residue subgroup. Every QNR factor is visible. If n=2 and a QNR exists, an atom supplies the nonidentity quotient coset and hits the order-two target. Thus with at least one QNR, **n>=6**. No safe-prime or prime-half group-order assumption was used.

These are established group mechanisms independently rederived in the T-only setting. They do not assert that all such h find a partition, that a finite h menu is universal, or that enough factor direction/mass must occur somewhere.

## Character routing and a T-only index-six refinement

For admissible p and T=(p+h)/4, reduction modulo h gives Legendre(T/h)=Legendre(p/h), since four is a nonzero square. Negative character means the total QNR prime valuation mass of T is odd, so at least one QNR occurs. For p=1 modulo 4, quadratic reciprocity also identifies Legendre(p/h) with Legendre(h/p). Reciprocity is not needed for the direct T-character identity.

Positive character does **not** imply all factors are QR: even QNR valuation mass may still hit -1. The negative-character route is a declared preference, not a rejection gate. Any code treating unpreferred rows as mathematical misses would violate the proof and matched-control contract.

An exact consequence for a miss with negative character and n=6: visible quotient orders cannot be one, two or three (the order-three interval for e>=1 would saturate its subgroup). In C6, therefore every visible factor has order six and is QNR. The mass bound says V<=2, while character forces V odd; hence **V=1**, and S/H consists exactly of {1,rH,r^-1H}, three of six classes. This differs from an N=pT binary box containing an additional distinguished QNR factor p, which may have two visible units and five classes. Its classification must not be imported into T-only evidence. No novelty is assigned to this elementary corollary.

The Type-II construction remains the known [Tao/Vaughan coprime divisor parametrization, Proposition 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1107.1010v2): with coprime positive u,v, uv dividing T and h dividing u+v, set k=T/(uv), A=(u+v)/h and denominators (T,Apuk,Apvk). The exact identity follows directly. For the frozen p>h, T<p and T is not p-divisible; the other entries are p-divisible. Coprimality and odd h>=3 exclude u=v. Sorting gives three strict denominators and exactly two p-divisible entries. Repeated and unused valuations are permitted in k; coprimality with k or A is not required.

## Proof-only cross-shift transfer, not an expanded experiment

For distinct primes h,q both 3 modulo 4, if q divides T_h=(p+h)/4 and Legendre(q/h)=-1, then p is -h modulo q. Quadratic reciprocity gives Legendre(p/q)=-Legendre(h/q)=Legendre(q/h)=-1. Thus q is another admissible negative-character prime shift. Under h<p, the valid size statement is **q<=T_h<p**; q<T_h requires a separate compositeness/nonprime-divisor premise. The generic admissible miss h7,T47,p181 has q=T_h and demonstrates why strict inequality cannot be assumed from divisibility alone (a proof-boundary control outside the frozen residual targets).

The old preferred h31 miss supplies q=2053643, which is 3 modulo 4, and therefore an eligible transferred shift **outside** the frozen 107 ceiling. Its shifted row was not computed here. The newer p200023321 visible factor 531977 is 1 modulo 4 and cannot directly serve as this integral Type-II shift. Eligibility does not establish a hit; q can exceed h, so this is not a descending shift algorithm, termination proof or bound expansion. Reciprocal QNR arrows between h and q cannot form a two-cycle because their characters have opposite signs, but this alone does not prove general acyclicity.

The factorwise identity substantially overlaps [CENTL's shifted nonresidue transfer note](https://github.com/chasebryan/centl/blob/main/research/erdos-straus/SHIFTED-NONRESIDUE-TRANSFER.md), independently inspected as an author-published comparison.
