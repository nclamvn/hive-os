[Elsholtz–Tao v6, Proposition 2.3 and equations 2.6–2.8](https://arxiv.org/html/1107.1010v6).

has alpha_i+delta_i<=e_i and exponent j_i=2alpha_i+delta_i. All integers
0<=j_i<=2e_i occur: choose alpha_i=floor(j_i/2), delta_i=j_i modulo 2.
Then alpha_i+delta_i=ceil(j_i/2)<=e_i. Thus {a²d:ad|T}=Div(T²), and
squarefree d suffices for existence. This does not preserve all original

$$
\tau_{\mathrm I}=-(4T)^{-1}=-p^{-1}\pmod h,
\qquad \tau_{\mathrm{II}}=-1\pmod h.
$$
