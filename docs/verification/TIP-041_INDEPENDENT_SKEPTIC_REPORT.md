## 1. All-proper-subgroup sufficient proof

Let G=U(m), covered K constructible from already spent support J. For unused
disjoint block B, let D be generated by its factor cosets in G/K. Each signed
atom has digits [-e_i,e_i]. Its projection through H<=D has exactly
min(ord(r_iH),2e_i+1) distinct elements, independent of ambient cyclicity.

Suppose the full block image S is proper in D. Its exact stabilizer H is a
proper subgroup. In D/H, the final projected image is aperiodic; any prefix
period persists through multiplication by the remaining nonempty atoms, so
every projected prefix is also aperiodic. Iteratively applying Kneser gives

    |S/H| >= 1 + sum_i(min(ord(r_iH),2e_i+1)-1).

If the recorded inequality is at least |D/H| for EVERY proper H<D, this
contradicts properness. Therefore S=D. No cyclic D, primitive individual
generator, character sign or target membership assumption is required for
this coverage statement. The hypothetical stabilizer is only a proof device,
not an available covered subgroup or free witness budget.

If S=D, independently constructed legal B-representatives lift every D-coset.
Multiplying each by a separately paid old K-representation covers the preimage
L of D. Coordinates remain legal because J and B are disjoint. The block need
not contain -1: it may enlarge K, after which individual eligibility is restarted.
Only actual target membership in the constructively covered subgroup gives
a certificate. Strict Type-II root identities must still be checked directly.

This is a standard deduction from DeVos, Theorem1, not a novelty claim:
https://arxiv.org/html/1303.3539v1. Exact subgroup orders and all proper H are
essential. The identity check implies |D|<=2W+1 for THIS sufficient criterion;
neither this cap nor failed inequalities imply target absence. The condition
uses >= correctly; it does not weaken the distinct energy condition's strict >.
