PROBLEM
C04
For every finite simple graph \(G=(V,E)\) with \(V\ne\varnothing\) and every integer \(r\), if \(D(G,r)=0\), then \(r\in\{0,-2\}\). Here a graph is simple if its edges are unordered pairs of distinct vertices, a subset \(S\subseteq V\) is dominating if every vertex in \(V\setminus S\) is adjacent to some vertex of \(S\), and its domination polynomial is
\[
D(G,x)=\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq V\\ S\text{ is dominating}}}x^{|S|}.
\]
