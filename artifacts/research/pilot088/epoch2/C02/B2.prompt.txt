PROBLEM
C02
Let C be a set of ordered 5-tuples with entries in {0,1,2,3,4,5}. Require that any two distinct tuples in C have equal entries in at most one coordinate position. What is the largest possible cardinality of C?


REPLAYED RESPONSE 1
**C02.** I can rigorously establish
\[
\boxed{30\le \max |C|\le 36}.
\]
The exact maximum remains unresolved in this proof.

For the **upper bound**, project each tuple onto its first two coordinates. This projection is injective: two tuples with the same projection would agree in at least two positions. There are only \(6^2=36\) possible projections, so \(|C|\le36\).

For the **construction of 30 tuples**, use arithmetic modulo \(5\), with residues represented by \(0,1,2,3,4\). Start with
\[
D=\{(x,y,x+y,x+2y,x+3y):x,y\in\mathbb Z_5\}.
\]
Any two coordinate values uniquely determine \(x,y\), because the five corresponding linear forms are pairwise linearly independent. Consequently, distinct tuples in \(D\) agree in at most one coordinate.

Index the coordinate positions by \(\mathbb Z_5\). Remove the five tuples
\[
T_t=(t,t,2t,3t,4t),\qquad t\in\mathbb Z_5.
\]
For each \(t\), insert two new tuples:

- \(U_t\): replace the entries of \(T_t\) in positions \(t-1,t+1\) by \(5\);
- \(V_t\): replace the entries of \(T_t\) in positions \(t-2,t+2\) by \(5\).

All position indices are taken modulo \(5\). The resulting set contains
\[
25-5+10=30
\]
distinct tuples. We verify the agreement condition.

1. **Two retained tuples:** The condition follows from the property of \(D\).

2. **A retained tuple and a new tuple:** Replacing entries of \(T_t\) by \(5\) cannot create an agreement with a retained tuple, since retained tuples contain no \(5\). The retained tuple originally agreed with \(T_t\) in at most one position.

3. **The two new tuples associated with the same \(t\):** Their replacement-position sets are disjoint and together contain four positions. Thus \(U_t,V_t\) agree only in the remaining position.

4. **New tuples associated with different \(t\):** The tuples \(T_t,T_s\), for \(t\ne s\), differ in every position, since each coordinate is a nonzero multiple of the parameter modulo \(5\). Therefore, their modified tuples agree exactly where both have been replaced by \(5\). The ten replacement sets
   \[
   \{t-1,t+1\},\quad\{t-2,t+2\}\qquad(t\in\mathbb Z_5)
   \]
   are all distinct: each unordered pair of distinct positions has a unique midpoint \(t\), and its offsets from that midpoint are either \(\pm1\) or \(\pm2\). Two distinct two-element sets intersect in at most one position.

Hence this construction satisfies the requirement.

**Unresolved gap:** These arguments establish a valid size-\(30\) construction and the upper bound \(36\), but do not exclude sizes \(31\) through \(36\). Thus they do not determine the requested largest cardinality.
