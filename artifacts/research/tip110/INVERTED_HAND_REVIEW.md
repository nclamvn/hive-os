## 1. A full-stationarity identity valid in every apex order

Use I_j(t)=[A_j-jt,A_j-(j-1)t], width t, and L(t)=|union I_j(t)|.
At all but finitely many endpoint-event heights, put

    g_j(t)=1_{R_j exposed right boundary}-1_{L_j exposed left boundary}.

Distinct component boundaries suffice; generic endpoint ties occur at finitely
many heights. Persistent adjacent left/right parallel contact is separately
excluded at a competing minimum by the retained collision lemma. If a component
has left boundary label a and right boundary label b, its width derivative is
a-b+1. Hence, with k(t) the number of closed union components at generic t,

    L'(t)=k(t)-sum_j j*g_j(t).

The area derivative with respect to A_j is integral_0^1 g_j(t)dt. The usual
bounded difference quotients permit integration: union width is Lipschitz in
each horizontal offset, and the generic slice derivatives exist almost
everywhere. Thus genuine full-gradient stationarity gives

    L(1)=integral_0^1 k(t)dt,

since L(0)=0 even when several zero-width apex points are distinct. Combining
with retained radial stationarity yields

    2G=L(1)=integral_0^1 k(t)dt.

This does not give a target floor by itself. In the natural chart the accepted
cut/monotone-merging identity converts the component-count integral to one
plus the sum of cut closure heights. That transfer is NOT available for an
arbitrary inverted word. In particular this identity does not permit deleting
signed gap multiplicity or imposing consecutive original-label components.

## 2. Why a bare adjacent swap is not a slice-wise uncrossing theorem

For an inverted adjacent pair i,i+1, put u=A_i-A_(i+1)>0. The original inner
gap (R_(i+1),L_i) has constant width u at every t. Swapping the two apices
compresses the pair union, but its new internal coverage may be outside all
other triangles while the discarded outer wings were already covered. Thus
pair-union compression does not imply full-union compression.

The obstruction can be stated as an exact symbolic family, with no source
execution or area evaluation. Choose h,u,delta>0 satisfying

    u+delta<2h,                 u+4h<18/31,

and set

    p=(1+u+4h, u+2h+delta, 1+u+2h, 2h, h, 0).

All comparisons below follow from these strict inequalities; no parameter
search or numerical example is needed.

The apices are

    A=(2+u+4h, 2+u+2h+delta, 4+u+2h, 4+2h, 5+h, 6).

Their strict word is (2,1,4,3,5,6). For example A2<A1 follows from
delta<2h; A1<A4 follows from u+2h<2; A3<A5 follows from u+h<1. Thus this
is one of the retained disjoint-adjacent-swap words, not an arbitrary ordering.

The bottom minimum is p6=0, maximum p1=1+u+4h, with range<49/31. Sorted
bottom starts are

    0, h, 2h, u+2h+delta, 1+u+2h, 1+u+4h.

All successive gaps are strictly smaller than1: the critical middle gap is
1-delta. The closed bottom union is strictly connected, and its unique extreme
labels6 and1 give both one-sided depth slopes6. No radial/full-gradient
stationarity is claimed for this family.

At y=h, where width is1-h, intervals4,5,6 have the common left endpoint6h;

    (1+5h, 1+u+5h),           width u.

Now swap A3 and A4, keeping every other apex fixed. Equivalently p3 decreases
by u and p4 increases by u. The new interval3 starts at1+5h, while new

Thus the full slice union increases by u, although the pair's own union has

## 3. Exact missing stationary coupling

For a translation of a fixed label subset S, the within-S and within-S^c
areas are invariant. Actual G changes through the intersection of their two
unions. Full-gradient stationarity imposes zero first derivative of that
intersection, and a local minimum of G requires the appropriate nonnegative
second variations, with all contact/switch/bottom-tail contributions retained.
Pair intersection derivatives cannot be added as intersection-union
derivatives when several other triangles cover the same part.

The remaining requirement is therefore a whole stationary signed-exposure/
intersection-multiplicity argument proving a target value or an actual
full-space descending variation in every inverted continuum. The exact
component-count identity and pair compression do not furnish it. No complete
inversion exclusion or whole inverted target floor is asserted in this file.
