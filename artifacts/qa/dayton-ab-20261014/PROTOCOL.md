## One hypothesis

Across amendments and handover of one real dossier, existing HIVE revision,
citation and update facilities reduce operator work without increasing material
errors compared with the same Codex model using ordinary chat and generic tools.
This does not assume better model reasoning. One dossier with several rounds is

## Locked assessment dimensions

Before generation, enumerate every scored check and its expected answer from the
complete admissible pack in EXPECTED.md, then hash pack, task, key and protocol.
No new scored checks after reading arm outputs. Candidate subjects: controlling
deadline/version, superseded forms, performance thresholds, initial/full acceptance
dependency, cost omissions/double count, conditional vs mandatory requirements,
vendor evidence, ambiguous source wording and handover change traceability.

For each frozen check record PASS / FAIL / UNASSESSABLE with exact output and
source locations. Unassessable is not a pass. Critical errors include invented
vendor evidence, unsupported readiness/acceptance, wrong controlling deadline,
material missed mandatory submission requirement, or fabricated price/quantity.
Count both omissions and false-positive warnings, including HIVE's warnings.
Product heuristic score is not the test score or a factual verification.

Primary measures: critical error count and total active human work (including
import, JSON copying, warning triage, fixes, retrieval and handover). Secondary:
requirement accuracy, citation correctness, unintended edits, source retrieval
success, final artifact preservation, and logged tool/UI actions.
Agent wall-clock is not human work. Without a timed human operator/receiver,
human-time savings remain UNMEASURED. No simulated human-time claim.

The proposed commercial signal is at least 30% lower measured human work with no
additional critical errors and no lower requirement accuracy. Without those
measurements the hypothesis remains unestablished even if the demo looks good.
Use a label-blind assessor where feasible, disclose any unblinding and domain
review absence. Keep original outputs and failures. A tie remains a tie. One
positive case is not general product superiority; customer validation is separate.
