                                Q&A Responses – Tranche #2

Q#1: How many total users are anticipated to use the ERP system, and how are they broken
down by role or access level?

Answer: LACAHSA anticipates approximately 10 full-access users and 15 limited-access users

Q#2: How does LACAHSA currently manage its budgets?

Answer: Budgeting is currently managed entirely through Excel on a manual basis. LACAHSA is
seeking an automated solution.

Q#3: How does LACAHSA currently track grants and funding, and does LACAHSA act as a
grantee, grantor, or both?

Answer: LACAHSA operates exclusively as a grantor. LACAHSA receives revenue through
Measure A (a Los Angeles County sales tax measure approved in 2024) and passes through
approximately 70% of funds to 13 eligible jurisdictions. The remaining 30% (exclusive of a 5%
administrative allocation) is programmatic funding managed directly by LACAHSA, with a portion
awarded to sub-recipients who administer funds on LACAHSA’s behalf. Grant tracking is currently
handled through the Neighborly platform.

Q#4: Does LACAHSA use or intend to use a formal procurement or bid management system?

Answer: LACAHSA does not currently utilize a formal procurement or bid management system
but is in the process of hiring a Procurement Director who will evaluate and develop procurement
processes going forward. While not a current requirement of this RFP, LACAHSA is open to
solutions that support procurement automation and centralization.

Q#5: Is LACAHSA agnostic with respect to ERP vendor selection, or hiring based on certain
system skillsets?

Answer: Yes, LACAHSA is fully vendor agnostic. This is a true discovery process and no vendor
is currently considered a front-runner. Job postings for open roles have not been written to favor
any specific ERP platform. The ultimate ERP selection will be based on the outcome of this RFP
evaluation process.

Q#6: How many bank accounts does LACAHSA manage, and does LACAHSA currently have
direct bank connectivity with its financial system?

Answer: LACAHSA manages around 20 bank accounts across three banks: roughly 13 accounts
corresponding to each eligible jurisdiction and approximately 6 accounts for LACAHSA’s internal
operations. Of the three banks, two currently support bank feed connectivity, which has been set
up within the existing financial system (Sage Intacct).

Q#7: How does LACAHSA currently manage employee expense reports, and what is the
approximate monthly volume?

roughly 15–20 reports.

Q#8: What is LACAHSA’s current HR system, and are there plans to replace or integrate it?

Answer: LACAHSA currently uses BambooHR for human resources functions and requires
integration capability with the selected ERP. However, LACAHSA may be open to exploring
alternative solutions.

Q#9: What are LACAHSA’s current system integrations?

Answer: LACAHSA’s current integrations and connected systems include Bill.com (accounts
payable), Ramp (expense management), Power BI (reporting), and Neighborly (grant
management). LACAHSA is also exploring a future integration with Moody’s, which is used for
loan underwriting in connection with its Production, Preservation & Ownership (PPO) program.
LACAHSA is open to replacing Bill.com, Ramp, and other solutions if the selected ERP provides
comparable or improved functionality at a reasonable total cost.

Q#10: How many vendors/suppliers does LACAHSA currently have, and are you using any
automated scanning of supplier invoices today?

Answer: LACAHSA currently has approximately 50–75 active vendors. Vendor invoices are
submitted manually through Bill.com. Invoice approvals are also routed through Bill.com, with
approximately 10 individuals participating in approval chains.

Q#11: How many grants or funding recipients is LACAHSA currently managing?

Answer: LACAHSA is in the early stages of administering funding. Current funding recipients
include the 13 eligible jurisdictions, 9 awardees under LACAHSA’s most recent RFP,
approximately 20 TA recipients, and approximately 10 PPO-related developer distributions.

Q#12: How does LACAHSA currently handle financial and programmatic reporting, and is there
a business intelligence or reporting integration in place?

Answer: Financial reporting is performed within Sage Intacct, while programmatic reporting is
managed separately within the Neighborly platform. Reconciliation between the two systems is
currently a manual process. LACAHSA is in the early stages of exploring a Power BI integration
that would consolidate data from both systems.

Q#13: Does LACAHSA have fixed assets or leases that need to be tracked within the ERP?

Answer: LACAHSA does not currently have a significant number of fixed assets or leases,
however, LACAHSA is looking for a solution to track the various subscription-based IT
arrangements.

Q#14: The RFP includes property management and asset management as an optional ("should
have") requirement. Can LACAHSA elaborate on this need?

Answer: LACAHSA may own properties in the future, so is open to having the optionality and
capabilities within the system. Vendors may speak to the “should have” requirements at their
discretion, but should focus on the “must have” requirements.
