A maximum outside the interval between their positions removes only material on one side and leaves both endpoints in one recursive child. If the maximum is an interior letter larger than \(b\), the endpoints lie in different children. The output concatenates the left child's output before the right child's output, preserving the endpoints' original left-to-right order. Later concatenations cannot change their relative order. This accounts for outside letters and for either original ordering of the endpoints.
